## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a Computer Engineering graduate with professional experience in **Business Intelligence, Data Science, and Information Security**.

My work connects data analysis, business needs, and technology to support better decisions, improve processes, and develop useful digital solutions.

I enjoy exploring different areas of technology, from building analytical reports and automating workflows to working with databases, software development, and application security.

- 📊 **Business Intelligence:** dashboards, KPIs, reporting, and business insights.
- 🐍 **Data & Automation:** Python, SQL, data processing, and process automation.
- 🗄️ **Databases:** querying, transforming, and organizing data.
- 🔐 **Information Security:** security monitoring, vulnerability analysis, and governance.
- 🌐 **Software Development:** exploring web development and building practical applications.
- 🎓 **Education:** Computer Engineering — FIAP.

> My goal is to connect data, software, and business to transform real problems into practical solutions.
